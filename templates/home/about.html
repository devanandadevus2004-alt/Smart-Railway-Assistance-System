ABOUT THE PROJECT

Smart Railway Assistance and Reservation System

Making railway travel easier, safer and smarter.

About the System

The Smart Railway Assistance and Reservation System is designed to improve the overall railway travel experience for passengers through a smart and user-friendly platform.

The system brings together important railway services including ticket booking, intelligent seat allocation based on passenger preferences, luggage and parcel transfer, station guidance, emergency assistance and notifications.

The main objective of this project is to provide passengers with a more convenient and efficient way to access railway-related services from a single platform.

🚆

Our Goal

To build a smarter railway assistance system that focuses on passenger comfort, convenience, safety and efficient service delivery.
